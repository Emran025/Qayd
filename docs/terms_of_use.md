## 2. Nature of the Service

Users are responsible for understanding the legal implications of their financial commitments under their own governing law.

### 2.3 Encryption Architecture

Qayd employs end-to-end encryption across all user financial data. Your financial records — including vouchers, ledger entries, account balances, counterparty information, and transaction descriptions — are encrypted on your device using keys that only you possess. The Platform's servers route encrypted data between users but do not hold, derive, or have access to the decryption keys. This means that the Platform cannot read, inspect, modify, or reconstruct the financial content of your records at any time, for any reason, under any circumstances.

This is not a policy that the Platform chooses to follow. It is a structural property of the system's design.

When you sign a financial record within Qayd, the system produces a cryptographic digital signature using your private key over a deterministic representation of the record's financial substance (amount, currency, date, parties, and unique identifier).
